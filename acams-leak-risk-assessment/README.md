# ACAMS Proprietary Content Leakage — Board Risk Assessment

A defensive, Board-facing assessment of the risk that ACAMS proprietary content
(CAMS and advanced certification study materials, exam content, courseware,
standards, white papers, conference sessions) is exposed outside authorized
channels — and a demonstration of how an agentic-AI-driven program detects and
responds to that exposure.

## What's here

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `RISK_BRIEFING.md` | The Board narrative: why leakage matters, the six leak channels, likelihood/impact, the capability, and a mitigation roadmap with asks of the Board. |
| `METHODOLOGY.md` | The defensive detection playbook: authorization gate, public-source discovery, classification, severity scoring, catalog schema, and response workflow. |
| `dashboard.html` | An interactive exposure dashboard (published as a shareable artifact) showing findings by channel and severity, with a recommended action per item. |

## Important framing

This is a **rights-holder, defensive** assessment, scoped as follows:

- **Public sources only.** No circumvention of ACAMS or third-party access
  controls or paywalls.
- **Exposure, not redistribution.** Deliverables record *what* leaked, *where*,
  *how severe*, and *what to do* — they do not store or re-serve leaked content.
- **Authorized.** Intended to run under ACAMS sanction with Legal sign-off.
